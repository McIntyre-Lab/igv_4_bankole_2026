## IGV Browser for papers/bankole_2026

**Website:** https://bio.rc.ufl.edu/pub/mcintyre/sex_specific_splicing/igv/index.html

### Tracks
The IGV tracks provide a visual representation of transcript models,
splice junctions, and long read RNAseq alignments.

- visualize transcript models across Drosophila species
- visualize transcript models identified from long read RNAseq 
- compare transcript structures between males and females 
- examine splice junction usage 
- evaluate evidence supporting alternative splicing

### 
- **UJCanno:** Transcripts with fully annotated junctions (UJC in the five species annotation)
- **ERPanno:** Transcripts with annotated Exon Region Pattern and at least 1 novel junction (ERP in the five species annotation)
- **ERPnovel:** Transcripts with a novel Exon Region Pattern (neither UJC nor ERP in the five species annotation)
